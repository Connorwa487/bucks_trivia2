Buck's Night
bucks trivia

Setup

Arrange 16 shots in a square — 10 Fireball and 6 Apple Juice. Shuffle so nobody knows which is which.

Connor needs a premix or beer for the whole game.

How to Play

This is a pass and play game. The reader draws a question, reads it aloud, then passes the phone to the left after each round.

The reader cannot answer or steal.

Any player can call "MINE" at any point — even mid-sentence. Reader stops, player answers without hearing the rest.

Correct — pick any player to take a shot. Connor sips.

Incorrect — that player takes a shot. One other may call "STEAL". Max one steal per question.

Nobody calls MINE after two reads — group votes to skip.

Connor cannot be selected to take a shot.

Bonus Questions

Only available to the player who answered correctly.

Nail it — give out an extra shot. Connor takes a shot.

Miss it — take both shots yourself.

Connor's Drinks

Connor sips every correct first-attempt answer.

Connor takes a shot every correct bonus answer.

Who's Playing?

Add everyone except Connor.
